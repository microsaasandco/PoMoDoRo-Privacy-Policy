# Privacy Policy – MicroSaaS PoMoDoRo

MicroSaaS PoMoDoRo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information to third parties. All data (sessions, settings) is stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and never leaves your device.

- No data is sent to external servers.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- No personal information is required or used.

For any questions, contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing.
