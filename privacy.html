MicroSaaS&Co PoMoDoRo
Privacy Policy
Last Updated: May 23, 2026

1. Introduction

MicroSaaS PoMoDoRo ("the Extension") is a Chrome extension that helps you manage your time and productivity using the Pomodoro technique. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

2. Data We Collect

The Extension collects and stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Pomodoro Sessions: Timer states, durations, and session history
  • User Preferences: Settings such as durations, auto-start, notifications, chime, and UI options
  • Notification Preferences: Your choices for desktop notifications and in-app toasts

No personal or sensitive information is collected.

3. How We Store Data

All data is stored locally on your device only. We do NOT:

  • Send any data to external servers
  • Share data with third parties
  • Store data in the cloud
  • Track your activity
  • Collect analytics

Data is stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, managed by your browser.

4. Data Access & Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions:

  • storage: To save and retrieve your settings and session data locally
  • alarms: To manage Pomodoro timers in the background
  • notifications: To show desktop notifications for session transitions
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): To allow the extension to open its UI in a new tab

These permissions are used exclusively to provide the Extension's functionality. No data is sent outside your device.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Resetting or clearing your settings and session history in the Extension's interface
  • Clearing your browser's local storage (which will remove all Extension data)
  • Uninstalling the Extension (this will permanently delete all stored data)

6. Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and is protected by:

  • Your operating system's file system permissions
  • Chrome's built-in storage management
  • Your device's password/authentication

We do not transmit data over the internet, eliminating the risk of interception during transmission.

7. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share data with Google or any other organization.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Extension constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Extension's privacy practices, please reach out through the Chrome Web Store extension page.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of the date listed above.
